Concept D

Plan vs Actual

The relationship at the centre of this app is prescribed versus performed — that delta is what drives the grade, the progression, the fatigue flags and the score. So the whole interface becomes a diff. Every screen carries the same three-column spine at a different scale: plan, actual, delta — set, exercise, session, week, block. The set row stops being a form; the prescription is printed and you fill in the answer beside it. Delta is the only place strong colour is spent.
