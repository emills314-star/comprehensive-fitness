Concept C

Confidence

Every derived number in this app already carries a confidence, so the interface renders itself to look exactly as certain as the data is. Certainty becomes a visual dimension alongside hue: well-evidenced values are solid and filled, provisional ones are dashed outlines in muted ink. You can see what the app is sure about before reading a word — and evidence is never a disclosure, it is the always-visible second line of every claim.
