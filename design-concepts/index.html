Design exploration · not production

Four top-down designs for Comprehensive Fitness

Four complete reinterpretations of the app, each a different thesis about what it fundamentally is. Six screens apiece, rendered at 390 × 844. Nothing here touches the shipping app.

One rule: the palette does not change. Every concept loads tokens.css, a verbatim extract of the token block in index.html, and may only reference var(--token). capture.js fails the render if the tokens drift from the app or if any raw hex appears in a concept file — so palette fidelity is proved, not claimed.

The four directions

Concept A

Two-Speed

The app is used in two incompatible contexts, so it carries two visual languages. Gym is near-monochrome, spends one accent on the live state, and sizes numbers for arm's length. Study is dense and opens its evidence by default. Starting a workout changes the app's personality rather than navigating.

Best gym screen in the set · two systems to maintain Concept B

Budget

Training is allocation against research landmarks. One primitive — the allocation bar — repeats fractally at every scale: set in exercise, exercise in session, session in week, week in block. The bar is the container; navigation is a zoom level instead of four peer tabs.

Most elegant system · weakest fit for set logging Concept C

Confidence

Every derived value already carries a confidence, so the interface renders itself to look exactly as certain as the data is. Solid and filled where evidence is strong, dashed and muted where it is provisional — even the trend line goes dashed where the weeks are thin.

Most differentiated · lowest migration risk Concept D

Plan vs Actual

The central relationship is prescribed versus performed, and that delta drives the grade, the progression and the flags. So the whole interface becomes a diff: plan, actual, delta — at set, session, week and block scale. Delta is the only place strong colour is spent.

Most explanatory · tightest on a 390px phone
Recommendation

Concept C — Confidence is the strongest overall. It makes the app's genuine differentiator visible on every screen at no navigation cost, it is the hardest for any competitor to copy, and because it is a rendering rule layered over conventional information architecture rather than a new spatial model, it carries the lowest migration risk of the four.

Separately, Concept A's gym screen is the single best individual screen here. Whichever system wins, the active workout deserves that treatment: one accent, no disclosures, numbers you can read from arm's length.
