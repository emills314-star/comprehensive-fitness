Leaving an active workout

Hiding the navigation during a session is right — it is the gym-mode instinct, and it works. But the only exit currently sits in the footer after every exercise, so mid-session it is roughly three and a half screens away, and it gets further away the earlier you are in the workout. Three ways to fix that, in increasing order of how much they change.
