Concept A

Two-Speed

The app is used in two incompatible contexts, so it carries two visual languages instead of one compromise. Gym is near-monochrome, spends exactly one accent on the live state, and sizes numbers to be read at arm's length with no cards, disclosures, or navigation. Study is dense, layered, and opens its evidence by default. Starting a workout does not navigate — it changes the app's personality.
