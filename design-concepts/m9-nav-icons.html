Your question

Colour would hurt here.

Recommendation: keep the accent for the active tab and nothing more.
What per-tab colour does
Today Plan Progress More
Every hue already means something Green Progress reads as "done". Amber More reads as "warning". Gold Plan reads as "resting". These are not free colours — the whole app spends them on state, and the delta column depends on them being unambiguous.
Where colour does earn its place Only for state, never identity: the active tab in --current, exactly as now. Everything else in the nav stays neutral ink so the palette's meanings hold everywhere else.
One thing to fix while you are here The grip and overflow glyphs render faint at 22px because they use quieter contextual ink. That is a contrast issue, not a colour one — raise them to a token that clears AA against their surface.
The 4-tab nav is one of the few surfaces where the app has no state to express beyond "you are here". Spending hue there would cost more than it buys.
