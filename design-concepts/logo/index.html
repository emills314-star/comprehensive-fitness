Design exploration · not wired to the app

Five logo directions

Marks derived from what the app does rather than from gym equipment. Every colour is lifted from the shipped palette in index.html. Each mark is drawn inside the maskable safe circle, so the same geometry survives the rounded-square, circle and squircle masks. The in-app glyph is single-colour currentColor, so it restains itself in all ten colour packages.

01

Increment

Progression

The same lift, moved one step. Three identical pills stacked and offset by exactly one increment, brightening as they climb; the gold cap is the step you have not taken yet.
