Five lifting marks

Round two stays inside the equipment vocabulary: plate, bar, rack, lifter, bell. Same palette rule, same maskable safe circle, same one-colour in-app glyph. Each mark is drawn inside the maskable safe circle, so the same geometry survives the rounded-square, circle and squircle masks. The in-app glyph is single-colour currentColor, so it restains itself in all ten colour packages.

06

Plate

Bumper plate, face on

A single plate loaded on the sleeve, seen face on: deep body, bright colour ring near the rim, gold collar at the hub. The bar stubs are what stop a disc from reading as a dial - without them this is a steering wheel.

Rack

Bar in the hooks

The moment before the set: the bar racked high on two uprights, gold J-hooks holding it. The punched hole pattern is what makes it a rack rather than another barbell - drop the holes and it reads as a dumbbell again.

Kettlebell

One object

One object, one silhouette. Handle and body share a colour so the mark casts as a single shape with a real hole under the arch - a contrasting handle over a round body reads as a handbag, which is exactly what the first pass did.
