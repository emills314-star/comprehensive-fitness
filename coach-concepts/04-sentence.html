Your workout is one sentence the coach keeps rewriting.

How it feels

Like reading one very good paragraph that happens to be about you. A huge soft serif, wide margins, a single full stop to press. Nothing to scan and nothing to dismiss; the screen is always the sentence you are about to live.

How the coach speaks

As an editor, in the first person and plain words. It never opens a warning screen. It strikes a number inside your own sentence, writes the correction into it, and footnotes the evidence with your own figures.

The trade-off

Prose is slow to skim. You cannot compare five lifts at a glance the way a grid lets you, and a long session makes a long paragraph. It is built for the set in your hand, not for studying the week.
