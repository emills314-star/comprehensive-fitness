Coach concept 02 of 10

Earpiece

The coach talks; the screen is what it says.

How it feels
Phone on the bench, earbuds in. Poster-loud type you can read at arm's length, one sentence at a time. No scrolling, no dashboard: only the present moment.
How the coach speaks
Short, plain, numbered. “That was six. You normally lose less than one rep between sets — today you lost three.” The evidence arrives as a quiet strip while it talks, and it says how sure it is.
The trade-off
You need earbuds and a gym quiet enough to be heard. A sentence is slower than a glance at a chart, and there is no history: miss it and you tap Replay. Every spoken answer has a tap twin for the noisy days.
