05 Coach concept

Ambient

The best coach app is one you never open.

The coach lives on the surfaces you already glance at: the lock screen, the Dynamic Island, your wrist, your home screen. Six frames, six operating-system surfaces, no app screens. The app is one tap away, and only when you ask why.

How it feels
Like the phone itself knows how your session is going. The wallpaper carries the coach's mood: quiet blue while you are inside your normal, amber the instant you leave it. You log with one thumb from the lock screen and never unlock.
How the coach speaks
In glances. A number, a colour, a tap on the wrist: tap, tap, hold means "outside your normal". Long-press and you get a short recommendation, clearly labelled actions, and an honest "Open the coach app" if you want the full why. It cites your numbers, qualifies its causes, and never lectures.
The trade-off
Glanceable is also the ceiling. Live Activities carry a few hundred characters and the OS throttles how often they update. Anything that needs real thought, like editing the block or reading history, still needs the app, which this concept deliberately demotes to a fallback.

The ambient grammar

Edge light

The rim of the screen is the coach's mood, so a glance across the room is enough. Blue means live and inside your normal. Amber means a set left your normal and a decision is waiting. Plain means the coach has nothing to say.
