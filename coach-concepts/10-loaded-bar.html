Coach concept 10 of 10

Loaded Bar

The interface is the bar. Every lift is drawn as the real iron you are moving, loaded exactly right, and the coach coaches by physically changing what is on it.

How it feels

Chunky plates, knurled steel, stamped numerals. You never read a weight, you see it: 45 + 25 a side is two plates you could pick up. Loading the bar is the whole interface.

How the coach speaks

Like a spotter at your shoulder: short, specific, in your numbers. It hangs a tag on the collar, takes a plate off, and says why in one breath. No lectures, no jargon.

The trade-off

Loud and literal. Every machine, cable and dumbbell needs its own honest render, and bodyweight or band work has no plates to talk through. Ten rep pips are too narrow to be ten buttons, so they only show reps and the large minus and plus beside them do the tapping.
