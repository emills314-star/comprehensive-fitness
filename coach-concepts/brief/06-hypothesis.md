# 06 · Hypothesis — `coach-concepts/06-hypothesis.html`

**Thesis:** Every set is a prediction; every miss is a finding. The coach is a scientist who
designs each session as an experiment on you, states what it expects before you lift, and treats
a miss as evidence to explain — then designs the next test.

**Organizing primitive:** the predict → observe → explain → test loop. Before each set the coach
states a **prediction** with its range and basis ("Set 2: 8–9 reps · based on your last 12 sessions
and today's recovery"). Logging records the **observation** against it (Strong-fast: prefilled,
one tap). A miss opens a **finding** with competing explanations, each marked against your data as
*Supported*, *Not supported*, or *Untested* — never a fake probability. The next best action is
framed as a **test** with a pass condition.

**Frames:**
- F1 — Bench: prediction panel for set 2 (8–9 reps @ 185, RIR 2) above the logger; set 1 shown as
  "Predicted 9–10 · Observed 9 · Confirmed".
- F2 — Finding: "Prediction missed — observed 6, predicted 8–9." How far outside normal (drop of 3
  vs your 0.6 ± 0.7 → 3.4σ). Explanations as a ranked evidence table: Short rest — **Supported**
  (1:32 of 2:30) · Low recovery — **Supported** (HRV 38 vs 52, sleep 5 h 48 m) · Strength loss —
  **Not supported** (set-1 e1RM 253 lb within 4-week 249–254) · Pain or illness — **Untested** (ask
  → "Something hurts").
- F3 — Today's test: "Run 165 × 8 with the full 2:30 rest. **Pass:** 8 reps at RIR 2 → the drop was
  fatigue, not strength." Alternatives as weaker tests (185 + 3:00 → confounded; stop → no data).
  Downstream effect: incline 3 → 2 sets, weekly chest 15/16.
- F4 — Test running: set 3 at 165 × 8 with its prediction and pass condition, rest timer holding to
  2:30, incline shown as 2 sets.
- F5 — Thursday's experiment: hypothesis ("With 2:30 rest and normal recovery, 185 × 9+ on sets
  1–2"), protocol (hold 185 × 4 × 8–10, rest 2:30 enforced), success criteria (both ≥ 9 @ RIR ≥ 1 →
  190), stopping rule (second miss with normal recovery → pressing deload), and the HRV < 45 →
  175 protocol amendment.
- F6 (optional) — The coach's record: how often its predictions for you have been right (show the
  shape of a calibration, synthetic and modest).

**Feel:** a beautifully art-directed research figure — labelled panels (A, B, C), figure captions,
crisp rules, numbered references. Rigorous but warm; it should make the owner feel *understood*.

**Type:** Atkinson Hyperlegible + DM Mono.

**Must not become:** certainty rendered as solid/tinted/dashed visual weight (that was the
Confidence concept), or a lab notebook / ledger. This is the scientific method as structure.
