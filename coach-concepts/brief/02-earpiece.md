# 02 · Earpiece — `coach-concepts/02-earpiece.html`

**Thesis:** The coach talks; the screen is what it says. You lift with earbuds in and your phone
on the bench or in your pocket — the coach is a voice, and the screen exists to caption it and
catch one-tap answers.

**Organizing primitive:** the live caption. One spoken line at a time, set huge, with the word
being spoken highlighted (karaoke-style) and the line before it ghosted above. Beneath: a live
voice waveform and ONE enormous thumb target. Logging is by voice ("nine") or a single big tap on
prefilled reps; the screen shows the recognised words as chips. There is **no scrolling chat
history** — only the present moment.

**Frames:**
- F1 — "Set two. One-eighty-five, eight to ten. Two in the tank." Caption huge; data chips
  (185 lb · 8–10 · RIR 2 · prev 10) under it; the giant control: "Say your reps — or tap 9".
  Set 1 shown as a small logged chip "185 × 9 ✓".
- F2 — The flag arrives like an **incoming call**: a full-screen takeover ("Coach · 0:04",
  pulsing amber ring) that interrupts the rest screen. The caption speaks the finding in plain
  English ("That was six. You normally lose less than one rep between sets — today you lost
  three.") while number chips underneath carry the evidence (expected 8–9, 3.4σ, rest 1:32 of
  2:30, HRV −27%, e1RM 253 = trend). Two huge buttons: **Do it** / **Talk me through it**, plus a
  small red "Something hurts".
- F3 — Three huge spoken choices stacked as thumb-sized slabs, recommended first ("Drop to 165
  for two sets"), each with a one-line spoken consequence; caption at top reads the downstream
  effect ("Incline goes to two sets. Chest stays at fifteen of sixteen this week.").
- F4 — "Loading one-sixty-five. Swap each twenty-five for a ten and a five." — the rest countdown
  is spoken and shown as a giant number that will not release before 2:30; next set chip 165 × 8.
- F5 — "Here's Thursday." A spoken recap rendered as a short paragraph of captions with three
  chips (Hold 185 · Progress at 9+9 · HRV < 45 → 175) and a big Replay control.

**Feel:** bold, high-contrast, typographic, almost poster-like; motion implied by the waveform and
highlight. Reads at arm's length.

**Type:** Archivo (use its heavy/wide styles for captions) + Archivo Narrow for chips.

**Must not become:** chat bubbles or a message thread. One line, live, huge.
