# 10 · Loaded Bar — `coach-concepts/10-loaded-bar.html`

**Thesis:** The interface is the bar. Every movement is drawn as the literal equipment you are
using, loaded exactly as it should be, and the coach coaches by physically changing what's on it.

**Organizing primitive:** the equipment render. The barbell is drawn side-on with correct plate
math (45 lb bar; plates 45, 25, 10, 5, 2.5 sized and labelled distinctly using palette fills and
outlines). Reps are rep pips/tally marks along the sleeve or under the bar; tapping the bar (or a
big LOG pip row prefilled with the target) logs the set Strong-fast. Dumbbells render as a pair
("55s"), the pulldown as a pin-loaded weight stack with the pin at 140, the cable as its stack.

**Frames:**
- F1 — Bench bar loaded to 185 (per side 45 + 25), "8–10 @ RIR 2 · prev 10", set 1 logged as 9
  pips filled in success green, set 2's pips waiting (8 solid targets + 2 optional). Upcoming
  equipment peeking below (T-bar row, 55s dumbbells, stack at 140, cable at 15).
- F2 — The flag: set 2 shows 6 filled pips and **ghost pips** where reps 7–9 were expected; an amber
  coach tag hangs off the bar collar: "Outside your normal" with the numbers (expected 8–9 · drop of
  3 vs your 0.6 ± 0.7 · 3.4σ) and causes (rest 1:32/2:30, HRV −27%, sleep 5 h 48 m, not strength —
  e1RM 253 = trend).
- F3 — Three loaded bars stacked as options: **165 (recommended)** showing the plate change
  animated-in-place (each 25 swapped for 10 + 5 with arrows), **185 + rest 3:00**, and **rack it**
  (bar back in J-hooks). Downstream: the incline dumbbells show "2 sets" instead of 3; chest volume
  15/16. A small red "Something hurts".
- F4 — Bar reloaded to 165 (per side 45 + 10 + 5), set 3 pips ready (8), rest timer drawn as a
  collar/clamp countdown that won't release before 2:30.
- F5 — Thursday: the bar pre-loaded at 185 with a "HOLD" tag on the collar; a faint ghost of 190
  ("unlocks when sets 1–2 ≥ 9 @ RIR ≥ 1"); a second alternate bar at 175 labelled "if HRV < 45";
  and the deload watch rule as a small tag.

**Feel:** literal, physical, gym-honest — chunky plates, knurling texture, rack hardware, bold
signage typography. Plate math and loading are the hero; it should make loading the bar easier
than any app has.

**Type:** Bebas Neue for signage + Work Sans for text.

**Must not become:** a workbench/tool-inventory metaphor (Training Workshop) or a cartoon. It's
the real equipment, rendered beautifully.
