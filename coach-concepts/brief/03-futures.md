# 03 · Futures — `coach-concepts/03-futures.html`

**Thesis:** Every decision shows you the next session it creates. The coach doesn't issue orders —
it shows you two or three futures, projected through the rest of today and into Thursday, and you
pick the one you want to live in.

**Organizing primitive:** the projection. A persistent **projection rail** across the top of the
logger always shows where today's path lands next session ("On track: Thursday bench 190"). When
something changes, the futures fan out as parallel columns you can compare row by row. The key
interactive element is a **load scrubber**: drag today's back-off load and watch Thursday's
starting point, fatigue carried, and weekly chest volume re-project live (a small inline script
for this is welcome).

**Frames:**
- F1 — Logger (Strong-fast rows: set, previous, lb, reps, ✓) with set 1 logged 185 × 9; the
  projection rail reads "Thursday: 190 lb if sets 1–2 hit 9+".
- F2 — Set 2 = 6 and the rail **breaks**: "This set moved Thursday. Keep grinding at 185 and
  Thursday starts at 175." Expected 8–9 vs 6, 3.4σ beyond your 0.6 ± 0.7 usual drop, ranked
  causes (rest 1:32/2:30, HRV −27% / 5 h 48 m sleep, not strength — e1RM 253 = trend).
- F3 — Three futures side by side as tall columns: **A Back off to 165 × 8 ×2 (recommended)** /
  **B 185, rest 3:00, RIR 1** / **C Stop bench**. Shared rows: remaining sets today · weekly chest
  volume (15/16, 14/16-ish fatigue cost, 13/16) · fatigue carried into Thursday · Thursday bench
  start (185 hold / 180 / 185 but under-stimulated) · progression ETA to 190. The scrubber sits
  under column A. Choose / dismiss controls and the small "Something hurts" escape.
- F4 — Future A committed: logger updated (set 3 = 165 × 8, rest holding to 2:30, incline 2 sets),
  rail restored: "Thursday: hold 185".
- F5 — Thursday shown as **the future you chose**: the plan (hold 185 × 4 × 8–10, rest 2:30
  enforced), and the conditions that would fork it again (progress to 190 when sets 1–2 ≥ 9 @
  RIR ≥ 1; second flag with normal recovery → pressing deload; HRV < 45 → start 175).

**Feel:** forward-leaning, confident, a little cinematic — depth through layering of the futures,
the chosen one sharp and the others receding.

**Type:** Space Grotesk.

**Must not become:** a node graph, decision tree diagram, map, or route. Futures are parallel
columns and a live projection, not a graph.
