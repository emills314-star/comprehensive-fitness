# 04 · Sentence — `coach-concepts/04-sentence.html`

**Thesis:** Your workout is one sentence the coach keeps rewriting. No tables, no cards — the
whole session is set as large, beautiful, editable prose, and the coach coaches by editing it.

**Organizing primitive:** the editable sentence. Every value is an inline token ("Bench **185** for
**8–10**, **two** in reserve"). Tapping a token docks a Strong-fast stepper / number pad at the
bottom; tapping the sentence's full stop logs the set with the prefilled numbers. Logged sets turn
into past tense with a fine success underline ("You pressed 185 for 9.").

**Frames:**
- F1 — Today's paragraph: "Bench. You pressed 185 for 9. Now **185** for **8–10**, two in
  reserve." with a large tap-to-log full stop / end mark, the previous ("last week: 10") set as a
  quiet marginal gloss, and the rest of the session as lighter future-tense sentences below.
- F2 — The coach edits your prose in place, like beautifully typeset tracked changes: "You pressed
  185 for ~~8–10~~ **6**." flagged in amber, then an inserted clause: "That's three fewer than your
  first set — you usually drop less than one." A footnote mark ¹ leads to a foot note with the
  evidence (expected 8–9, 3.4σ, rest 1:32 of 2:30, HRV −27%, sleep 5 h 48 m, e1RM 253 = trend).
- F3 — The coach offers **three rewrites** of the rest of the paragraph, each a complete sentence
  you can pick (radio-style): "Finish with 165 for 8, twice, resting the full two and a half
  minutes. Incline becomes two sets." (recommended) / "Stay at 185, rest three minutes, stop one
  short of failure." / "Leave bench here and move to rows." Each with a one-clause consequence in
  smaller italic.
- F4 — The accepted paragraph, clean, with the strike-through history collapsed into a faint
  margin mark; the next token "165" active; rest shown as a clause that counts down ("Rest — 1:48
  more.").
- F5 — "Thursday." The next session written as a paragraph with conditional clauses: "Bench
  holds at 185 for four sets of 8–10, resting 2:30. When your first two sets both reach 9, we go
  to 190. If your HRV is under 45 that morning, start at 175. If bench slips again with normal
  recovery, pressing takes a light week."

**Feel:** literary, generous whitespace, huge display serif, quietly luxurious — the opposite of a
spreadsheet, while still logging in one tap.

**Type:** Fraunces (display serif, use its optical size/soft axes) + Inter for the docked keypad.

**Must not become:** a chat, a command line, a newspaper or magazine layout. It is one living
paragraph.
