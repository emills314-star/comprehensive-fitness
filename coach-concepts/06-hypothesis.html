06Hypothesis

Every set is a prediction;
every miss is a finding.

The coach is a scientist. It designs each session as an experiment on you, says what it expects before you lift, and treats a miss as evidence to explain, then designs the next test.

How it feels
Like reading a beautifully set research figure about yourself: lettered panels, hairline rules, captions, numbered sources. Rigorous, and plainly on your side.
How the coach speaks
First person, in your numbers: “I predicted 8–9. You got 6.” It owns its misses and marks every explanation Supported, Not supported or Untested. Never an invented percentage.
The trade-off
More to read at the exact moment a set goes wrong, and “Untested” is honest but clinical on a bad day. A prediction is only as good as your last 12 sessions.
  1. IPredict

    Before a set, the coach states a range and what it is based on.

  2. IIObserve

    You log it, Strong-fast. The result is held against the range.

  3. IIIExplain

    A miss opens a finding, with each cause marked against your data.

  4. IVTest

    The next best action is a test, with what a result would mean written down first.
