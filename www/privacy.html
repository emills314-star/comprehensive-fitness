Comprehensive Fitness is a local-first workout tracker. The app stores workout sessions, exercise names, sets, reps, weights, RPE, notes, and settings on your device.

Data Collection

The app does not require an account. Workout history, routines, settings, readiness entries, and imports are stored locally in IndexedDB. The app does not upload workout history.

If you explicitly enable background rest notifications, scheduling an alert sends the installation and timer identifiers, workout, exercise and set identifiers, exercise name, upcoming-set label or number, and completion time needed to deliver and route that alert. The Private notification-detail option changes only the visible notification body; those server-side timer fields are still transmitted.

Backups and Strong CSV files are selected by you and validated locally before import. A hosted app does not probe private file paths, and private responses are not cached.

Third Parties

The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers. Optional background notifications use Vercel Functions, Upstash Redis, Upstash QStash, and the browser push service. Push endpoints, encryption keys, and requested timer records are used to deliver requested alerts. The app has no account, cloud-history, workout-upload, or cloud-restore service.

Your Control

You can export your data, import a validated backup, disable background notifications, clear local data, or request deletion of this installation's remote notification records and any legacy workout-copy records from Settings. Full remote deletion preserves local workouts and may continue through a visible retry state until bounded server cleanup is confirmed. Local clearing pauses while required remote deletion authorization or an unresolved active-timer cancellation must be retained. A downloaded app-data export is the implemented recoverable copy.

Training Guidance

Progression recommendations are informational fitness guidance. The app does not diagnose medical conditions or provide medical treatment. Stop for severe, unexplained, or worsening pain and consult a qualified professional before making health or training decisions.
